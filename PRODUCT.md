# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Young adults (18–35) reaching for their phone late at night, mid-emotion — unsure if they love or miss someone, wondering if they're healing or just coping. Not seekers of clinical therapy; people who want honest self-knowledge without jargon or judgment.

## Product Purpose

Shugi offers two mirrors: an AI companion (Shugi AI) for unstructured emotional conversation, and 20 validated psychology quizzes for structured self-assessment. Success means someone leaves with more clarity than they arrived with — not fixed, but less alone in their confusion.

## Brand Personality

Intimate, clear-eyed, unhurried. The antithesis of the wellness-app aesthetic. A wise friend who studied psychology and speaks without performing.

## Anti-references

- Headspace / Calm (pastel soft-launch optimism — assumes the user is already OK)
- BetterHelp (clinical framing, therapy-as-product marketing)
- Generic mental health startup (white + teal reflex, smiling stock photos)
- Galaxy-brain wellness (crystal energy, chakra iconography, "manifest your healing")

## Design Principles

1. **Intimacy over scale** — this is a private, late-night experience. Design for one person, not an audience.
2. **Clarity is care** — the interface must add zero cognitive load to someone already overwhelmed.
3. **Earned trust** — clinical tools (PHQ-9, GAD-7, PCL-5) are real. The design must honor their weight without feeling medical.
4. **Dark is not decoration** — the dark palette fits the context. It should feel deliberate, not edgy or gaming-adjacent.
5. **Words are the medium** — questions, answers, reflections. Typography carries the weight of the experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Color is never the sole information carrier. Text legibility at low brightness (phone screen at night) is a primary constraint.
